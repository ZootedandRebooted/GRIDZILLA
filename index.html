From SD to your next beat

GRIDZILLA is an SD-based custom firmware-style mod. It extends the MPC Sample workflow without flashing or replacing Akai’s firmware.

  1. Save and back up.

    Save your project, back up your SD card, then power off the MPC before removing the card.

  2. Unzip and copy.

    Follow the guide. Merge autorun and WaveGrid into the SD root, keeping your existing projects and samples.

  3. Eject and start.

    Safely eject the card, put it back in the powered-off MPC, then start up. Already installed? Follow the guide’s update steps.
